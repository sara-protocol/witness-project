# Code of Conduct

- Assume good faith. Disagree on evidence and logic, not on persons.
- Present counter-evidence fairly. Do not strawman opposing theories.
- Do not claim a contested interpretation as established fact.
- Respect traditions: present Buddhist, Vedanta, Zhuangzi and other sources accurately, with primary texts where possible, without裁决 where the project has not decided.
- No harassment, no personal attacks, no spam, no promotion of unrelated agendas.
- Maintainers may close Issues / PRs that violate this code, with a public reason.
